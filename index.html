Module 1 · Living World System

Weather that remembers your rhythm

Touch, drag, or tap anywhere on the stage. Fast motions push wind and streaks; lingering touches slow clouds, bring back light, and draw creatures out. The world reacts with delay and inertia—no instant reward, only a slow shift you can sense.

The stage listens to every module. The more you play elsewhere, the more this world morphs on its own.

Module 2A · Morph Creatures

Stretchable creatures that learn your tempo

Pull, squish, and swirl the blobs. They record your usual pace and pulse at the rhythm you set. No scores—just a mirror of how you move.

Module 2B · Rhythm Pool

Particles that follow with delay

Move your pointer or finger over the pool. Particles chase you with damping and inertia—quick paths scatter, steady paths converge. The pool also feeds the world.

Guide slowly, then abruptly, and watch how long the pool takes to settle.

Module 3 · Chaos Lab

Make chaos, then reel it back

Splash color, tear space, bend sound. The mess never escapes a safety threshold; the louder you go, the slower the lab responds, forcing you to guide it back.

Module 4 · World Repair

Stand near, stay still, watch it mend

Fractures seal themselves when you linger nearby. No tasks, no counter—just slower motion that lets the landscape tidy itself.

Module 6 · Playful Sound World

Tap tones that auto-align

Each pad drops a tone onto a shared pulse. Rapid taps get softened by filters, guiding you into a slower groove without forcing it. Layer sounds, drag the slider to stretch the beat.
